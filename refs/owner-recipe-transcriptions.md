## 1. Black Bean & Lentil Soup  [MAIN]
1 Tbsp olive oil; 2 cloves garlic, minced; 1 onion, diced; 2 carrots, diced; 1 c. lentils;
1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes; 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained; 4 c. vegetable broth;
1 tsp chili powder; 1/2 tsp cumin; 1/2 tsp salt; 1/2 tsp pepper
Method: Saute onion & carrots in olive oil until onion soft. Add garlic, 1 min. Add remaining,
stir, cover. Simmer until lentils & carrots tender (25-30 min). Serve with bread or crackers.
COOK ~30 min. Vegan, gluten-free.

## 3. Baked Turkey Meatballs  [MAIN]
1 lb ground turkey; 1 egg; 1/2 cup bread crumbs; 1/4 cup chopped parsley; 1 clove garlic,
finely chopped; 1/4 cup onion, finely diced; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 1 tsp salt; 1 tsp pepper
Method: Mix all with hands. Shape into meatballs, place on greased pan. Bake 400F 20 min,
until golden brown & not pink in middle. Serve with desired sauce and sides. Makes 20 meatballs.
COOK 20 min. YIELD 20 meatballs (servings still unknown).

## 4. Balsamic Chicken & Veggies  [MAIN]
1/4 cup + 2 tbsp italian dressing; 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar; 1 1/2 tbsp honey;
1/8 tsp red pepper flakes; 1 1/4 lbs chicken tenderloins; 1 lb fresh green beans, chopped 2in;
1 1/2 cups matchstick carrots; 1 cup halved grape tomatoes; 2 tbsp coconut oil
Method: Whisk dressing, vinegar, honey, red pepper; set aside. Heat oil in skillet med-high.
Season chicken salt & pepper; cook 6-7 min, rotating once halfway. Chop green beans & tomatoes
while chicken cooks. Add half dressing to skillet, toss chicken to coat. Remove chicken, add
vegetables (minus tomatoes). Season, cook until crisp tender ~4 min. Remove veggies, add
remaining sauce, cook until thickened ~1 min. Return chicken & veggies, toss to coat.
NOTES: Asparagus can be used in place of chicken. Coconut oil can be substituted as well.
COOK ~15 min.

## 5. Broccoli Soup  [MAIN]
3 c. vegetable or chicken stock; 2 c. broccoli florets; 1 small diced onion;
1 (15 oz) can evaporated milk; 2 c. shredded cheddar cheese; salt & pepper to taste
Method: Stir stock, onion & broccoli in medium pot. Boil until broccoli & onion tender.
Remove broccoli to blender, blend to desired texture. Add back to pot with evaporated milk,
bring to simmer. Remove from heat, stir in cheese. Season to taste.
NOTE: "salt & pepper to taste" -> Quantity::Unknown.

## 6. Chicken Parmesan Bake  [MAIN]
3 chicken breasts; 1 cup marinara sauce; 4 slices provolone cheese; 1/4 cup bread crumbs;
1/4 cup parmesan cheese
Method: Spray baking dish, place chicken in bottom. Cover with sauce; place cheese slices over
top. In separate bowl mix breadcrumbs & parmesan; sprinkle evenly over dish. Bake 350F 35-45 min
until chicken cooked through. Serve with pasta or spaghetti squash.
NOTES: Chicken tenderloins can replace breasts. Mozzarella can replace provolone. Sauce amount
adjustable to taste.  COOK 35-45 min.

## 9. Crockpot Creamy Chicken & Wild Rice Soup  [MAIN]
1 1/2 cups uncooked wild/brown rice blend; 1 1/2 lbs boneless skinless chicken (breast or thigh);
1 large onion, diced (~1 cup); 2 cups chopped celery; 2 cups chopped carrots; 2 bay leaves;
2 tsp dried parsley; 1/2 tsp dried thyme; 1/2 tsp ground sage; 1/2 tsp garlic powder;
1/2 tsp black pepper; 1/2 tsp salt; 4 cups chicken stock; 3 cups water; 1/2 cup heavy cream
Method: Combine all except cream in large crockpot. Cook low 6-8 hrs. Remove & shred chicken;
return. Remove bay leaves. In separate bowl slowly whisk 1-2 cups soup into heavy cream. Pour
into crockpot & whisk.
NOTES: For thinner soup use less rice. Vegetables can be substituted. If using frozen chicken,
thaw before cooking.  COOK 6-8 hrs.

## 10. Crockpot Mac & Cheese  [MAIN]
8 oz elbow macaroni, cooked; 2 eggs, beaten; 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk; 1 1/2 cups milk;
3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese; 1/2 cup butter, melted; 1 tsp salt; pepper to taste
Method: Gently mix all in large bowl until well combined. Pour into greased crockpot.
Cook low 3-4 hrs until hot & bubbly.
NOTE: For tighter mac & cheese use 1 egg, 1/2 c. milk, 1/4 c. butter.  COOK 3-4 hrs.

## 13. Easy Shepherd's Pie  [MAIN]
1.5-2 lbs potatoes; 8 tbs butter; 1 medium onion, chopped (~1.5 cups); 2 cups mixed vegetables;
1.5 lbs ground beef (ground turkey can be used); 1/2 cup beef broth; 1 tsp worcestershire sauce;
salt, pepper, seasonings of choice
Method: Cook potatoes until tender, set aside. In large skillet melt 4 tbs butter, cook onions
until tender (6-10 min). Add mixed vegetables & saute until tender. Add ground beef, season,
cook until no longer pink. Add broth & worcestershire, simmer uncovered 10 min, adding broth as
needed to prevent drying. Spread meat & vegetables in a large (9x13) dish. Mash potatoes with
4 tbs butter & seasonings; spread on top. Bake 400F 30 min until browned & bubbling.
COOK ~60 min total.  NOTE: card says "(8x13)" but standard is 9x13.

## 14. Egg Roll in a Bowl  [MAIN]
1 lb ground turkey; 1 onion, chopped; 1 tbsp vegetable oil; 1 tsp sesame oil;
16 oz coleslaw mix; 6 oz ramen or egg noodles; salt & pepper to taste
SAUCE: 1/3 c. soy sauce; 1 tbsp honey; 1 tbsp rice vinegar; 1 clove minced garlic
Method: Whisk sauce ingredients, set aside. Heat vegetable & sesame oil in skillet over medium
heat, saute onions until tender. Add ground meat & cook until brown & cooked through. Add
coleslaw mix & cook until cabbage softened. Cook noodles until tender, drain, add to skillet.
Add sauce & toss to combine.   COOK ~20 min.

## 16. Potato Kale Soup  [MAIN]

## 17. Potato Soup  [MAIN — CROCKPOT]
4 c. peeled & diced potatoes; 1 chopped onion; 3 c. chicken broth; 4 tbsp butter; 1/4 c. flour;
1/4 c. sour cream; 1 1/2 c. heavy cream; salt & pepper to taste
Method: Add potatoes, onion & chicken broth to crockpot, cook low 4-6 hrs or high 3-4 hrs until
potatoes tender. 30 min prior to serving melt butter in saucepan, whisk in flour, cook until
bubbly. Slowly stir in cream & sour cream. Add mixture to soup & cook 20-30 min. Serve with
desired toppings.
CROCKPOT -> can leave -> active time only (~15 prep + 30 finishing = ~45).
