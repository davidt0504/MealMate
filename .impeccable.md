## Design Context

Gathered 2026-09-05 during the first-run design interview and extended 2026-09-08. Palette
and type remain under in-app evaluation (appearance lab); the shortlist below is the candidate
set, not the decision.

### Users

The adult carrying a household's recurring food-planning load — the archetype is a tired
single parent who installed the app at 6pm to hand off "what's for dinner" and "what do I
buy". One-handed, dim kitchen, interrupted. They want the answer first and will give more
information only when they reach for it. Two phones are two households; no accounts.

### Brand Personality

A calm, confident woman who is also a warm, reassuring mother figure. Quiet confidence,
no urgency, no cheer. 決 — it is decided. Not another cozy food app: the warmth lives in
the voice ("we couldn't check", "never suggest shrimp?"), in generous leading and in one
warm accent, never in illustration, pastel or celebration.

Three words: settled, unhurried, warm.

### Aesthetic Direction

Japanese minimalism as working vocabulary — *kanso* (omit), *ma* (space is the product),
*shibui* (understated), *shizen* (every neutral tinted; no pure white or black). The home
state is a sentence, not a dashboard; exceptions are the only ink.

Palette candidates (light default; dark is the evening variant of the same tokens):
1. Aizome indigo ink · unbleached linen ground · persimmon/amber accent (leading candidate)
2. Lockup literal: navy · cream · amber
3. Sumi near-black · washi paper · persimmon, rare
4. Evening kitchen: cream text on deep indigo · amber (as a default only if it wins on-phone)
5. Tea garden: deep tea-leaf green · rice paper · roasted persimmon
6. Rainy veranda: composed rain blue · warm plaster · clay
7. Cedar · clay: cedar-brown ink · warm paper · fired earth

The 2026-09-08 extension uses Fumi of
[Speak Japanese Naturally](https://www.youtube.com/@SpeakJapaneseNaturally) as a human reference
for calm warmth paired with evident competence. It does not copy the channel's graphics or colors:
warmth stays in humane neutrals and the rare earthen accent, while confidence comes from dark,
low-chroma ink colors, legible hierarchy, and restraint.

Accent is ≤10% of visual weight: Accept and the "needs you" count, little else. No alarm
red — hard constraints are stated in ink. Brand lockup: `docs/brand/`.

Type candidates (all Google Fonts, bundled as Latin subsets; no runtime fetch — release
carries no INTERNET permission):
- Shippori Mincho or Zen Old Mincho display + Atkinson Hyperlegible body (leading)
- Zen Maru Gothic display + Atkinson Hyperlegible body
- Atkinson Hyperlegible alone, hierarchy by size and weight

Identity comes from the app's own principles, not from anti-references.

### Design Principles

1. Answer first, evidence below. The first thing on any screen is the conclusion.
2. Empty space is the deliverable. A covered week is quiet; do not fill it.
3. Correct by reacting, never by configuring. Swap, veto and restriction prompts live on
   the result; no questionnaire, no tutorial overlay. If a screen needs a tutorial, the
   screen is wrong.
4. Warmth by voice. Copy speaks as "we", plainly, and never claims safety (invariant 19).
5. Motion only on state change; nothing bounces, nothing celebrates (PRD principle 13).
6. WCAG AA contrast, system font scaling to 200%, reduced motion honored.
